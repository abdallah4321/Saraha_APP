Overview
Saraha App is a secure and anonymous messaging platform that allows users to send and receive messages without revealing their identity. Inspired by the traditional concept of "Saraha" (meaning "openness" or "frankness" in Arabic), this app encourages honest and candid communication while maintaining user privacy.

Features
Anonymous Messaging: Send and receive messages without revealing your identity.

User Profiles: Create a unique profile link to share with others for receiving messages.

Message Management: View, delete, or block messages from specific users.

Privacy Controls: Customize your privacy settings to control who can message you.

Responsive Design: Access the app seamlessly on both desktop and mobile devices.

Secure: End-to-end encryption ensures your messages are private and secure.


Usage
Create a Profile: Sign up and create a unique profile link.

Share Your Link: Share your profile link with friends or on social media.

Receive Messages: Others can send you anonymous messages via your profile link.

Manage Messages: View, delete, or block messages as needed.
